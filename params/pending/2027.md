# 2027: what is known so far

**Status (2026-10-05):** not loaded by the app. The CRA has not yet published T4127-JAN-2027. It usually comes out in November or December. Until then the calculator tells students that 2027 rates aren't published yet, instead of guessing.

Once T4127-JAN-2027 is out, follow "Adding next year's parameters" in the README. Use the figures below as a cross-check: each must match the CRA files, and any mismatch should be flagged rather than resolved silently.

Derived from the CPP changes:

- **Total employee CPP rate:** 5.75%
- **Schedule 8 base share:** 4.75 / 5.75 ≈ 82.6087%. Check this against the 2027 Schedule 8 line 9 percentage.
- **The `0.0495 / 0.0595` ratios in T4127** become `0.0475 / 0.0575`. These ratios are read from the params, so no code changes are needed.

## Still to come

- YMPE and YAMPE. The CRA announces these in early November. Third-party estimates are around $76,900 and $87,600; do not use them.
- The maximum base and total CPP contributions, which depend on the YMPE.
- The federal indexation factor: brackets, basic personal amount, Canada employment amount.
- The Ontario indexation factor: brackets, basic personal amount, surtax thresholds, tax-reduction basic amount.
- 2027 forms for the annual engine: Schedule 8, T2204, Schedule 11, ON428 and ON428-A, including the LIFT amounts.
